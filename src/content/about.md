# About

Explore public library use in England: loans, visits, events, computers, Wi-Fi and active borrowers.

The dashboard is maintained by [Libraries Hacked](https://www.librarieshacked.org), an open data project about public libraries.

## Data source

The data is published annually by [Arts Council England (ACE)](https://www.artscouncil.org.uk/supporting-arts-museums-and-libraries/supporting-libraries) as the Public Libraries Activity Dataset.

The dataset includes:

* **Loans:** Books, audiobooks, ebooks and digital audio, grouped by the item's intended age group.
* **Visits:** Visits to physical libraries, mobile libraries, and home deliveries.
* **Events and attendance:** In-person and online activities, and how many people attended.
* **Computers and Wi-Fi:** Public computer hours and wireless internet sessions.
* **Active users:** Members who borrowed or renewed at least one item during the year.

Not every service supplies every figure each year.

## Population comparisons

Areas have different population sizes, so rates help you compare them:

* Rates use the populations and months with reported data, based on Office for National Statistics (ONS) mid-year population estimates. Yearly rates scale the available months to a full year; missing activity is not known.
* Compare services, regions or a service's ONS Nearest Neighbours: areas with similar characteristics.

Rates do not make services alike. Geography, visitors from other areas, opening hours, counting methods and missing data still matter. Active users count borrowers, not everyone using a library.

## Data quality

Local authorities supply their own figures. These can contain errors, gaps or different counting methods.

* Switch between reported figures and corrected estimates.
* Issues, corrections and excluded figures are listed in the public [audit register](https://github.com/LibrariesHacked/public-libraries-activity-dataset/blob/master/data/errors.csv).
