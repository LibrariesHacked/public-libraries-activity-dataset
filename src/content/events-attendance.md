These charts show **events held** and **attendance** each month per 1,000 residents in areas with reported data. Each compares in-person and online events on its own scale.

Do months with more events also have higher attendance? Does the pattern repeat each year? Different services may report events and attendance, so dividing the chart rates will not give average turnout. Changes may reflect who reported, and totals spread evenly across months can hide seasonal patterns. A person attending twice counts twice.
