Visits include trips to library buildings, whether or not people borrow, plus mobile-library and home-delivery interactions. Repeat visits count separately. The cards show totals for the selected months; branch and mobile activity are part of the overall total.

In the full 2025/26 dataset, most reported visits were to library buildings. Mobile libraries and home delivery recorded far fewer interactions.

Card rates use the months reported to estimate a yearly rate per resident. Changes in share show how activity is split, in percentage points, not whether visitor numbers grew. Year-on-year changes cover the full financial year, not just your selected months.
