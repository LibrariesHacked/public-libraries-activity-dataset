Events are scheduled library activities, in person or online, with at least one attendee. Attendance counts visits to events, not bookings or individual people across the year. The cards show totals for your selected months.

Almost all reported events in the corrected 2025/26 dataset were in person.

Average turnout uses only records with both events and attendance for the same service, month, event type and age group. Large events can pull up the average, so it need not describe a typical event.

The under-18 share includes under-12s and 12-17s, as a share of all attendance, including unknown ages. Changes in this share are in percentage points, not growth in attendance. Card changes compare financial years.
