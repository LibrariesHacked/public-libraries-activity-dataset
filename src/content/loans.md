A loan counts each time an item is borrowed or renewed. The cards show totals for your selected months. Physical books and digital loans are parts of the total; under-18 books are included in physical books.

Physical books made up about 82% of reported loans in the corrected 2025/26 dataset.

Rates use the populations and months with reported data. The digital share shows the split of recorded loans, not demand for digital reading; missing formats can affect it. Changes in share are in percentage points; other changes refer to loan counts. All changes compare financial years, not just your selected months.
