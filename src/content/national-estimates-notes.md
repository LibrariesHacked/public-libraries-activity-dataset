**Population-based estimate:** Not every library service reports every measure. To estimate the total for England, we divide the reported total by the population covered by services that reported, then multiply by England's population:

`(Reported total ÷ Reporting population) × England population`

**70% coverage threshold:** We flag an estimate with a caution note when both the share of services reporting and the share of England's population represented are below 70%.

**Source:** The method follows the DCMS report, [Secondary Data Analysis of Arts Council England’s English Public Libraries Activity Dataset 23/24](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/secondary-data-analysis-of-arts-council-englands-english-public-libraries-activity-dataset-2324).
