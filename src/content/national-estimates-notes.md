### How estimates are calculated

Divide the reported total by the population of reporting areas, then multiply by England's population, using ONS mid-2024 estimates.

`(Reported total ÷ Reporting population) × England population`

### Coverage

* **Benchmark:** DCMS uses at least 70% population coverage or 107 of 153 library services.
* **Figures left out:** Yearly totals marked Corrected or Excluded are not used here. Activity pages use available corrections when switched on, so the two views can include different services.
* **Missing data:** Fewer reporting services make estimates less certain.

Estimates assume similar activity per resident in areas with and without data. Meeting the benchmark does not prove this is true or tell us the margin of error. Detailed numbers are still estimates.

### Source

The method follows the DCMS publication, [Secondary Data Analysis of Arts Council England’s English Public Libraries Activity Dataset 23/24](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/secondary-data-analysis-of-arts-council-englands-english-public-libraries-activity-dataset-2324).
