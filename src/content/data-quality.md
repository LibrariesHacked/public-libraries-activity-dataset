# Data quality and audit register

The **Public Libraries Activity Dataset** shows figures from the official survey collected and published by [Arts Council England (ACE)](https://www.artscouncil.org.uk/supporting-arts-museums-and-libraries/supporting-libraries).

Each library service submits its own figures, so reports may contain mistakes or unusual values. We do not change or remove these figures without explanation. The public register below records every known issue and change.

## Types of issue

* **Excluded:** Figures that are clearly impossible, such as millions of computer hours in one month. We leave these out of national totals so they do not skew the results.
* **Corrected:** Clear typing or unit mistakes where the intended figure is known, such as reporting minutes of computer use as hours or adding an extra zero. Choose **Original data** or **Corrected data** in the dashboard to see either version.
* **Standardised:** Some services report quarterly totals instead of monthly figures. We divide each total evenly across the three months to avoid misleading jumps and gaps in the charts.
* **Notes:** We keep unusual figures when we cannot confirm they are wrong, and add an explanation. For example, a service may report all registered members instead of members who used the library in the last 12 months.

The three-year source audit also checks category totals, published annual totals against supplied months, month-to-month outliers and large year-to-year changes. These checks identify figures to investigate, not confirmed mistakes. Relevant respondent explanations are included in the notes, including estimated interaction counts, service closures and changes in reporting coverage. An explained fall in activity is not automatically treated as a typing error.

The source workbooks contain some misleading labels and incomplete classifications. We check assignments against the original monthly question descriptions and formulas rather than assuming annual labels are correct. Missing data are not the same as reported zeroes. Arts Council England's exclusions from its headline tables can also include verified extreme values and are not necessarily the same as this dashboard's exclusions of impossible figures.
