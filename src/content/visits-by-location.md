Each chart shows monthly visits or interactions for one location type per 1,000 residents in areas with reported data. Each has its own scale so smaller services remain visible.

**Library** and **Shared building** count building visits. **Mobile library** and **Home delivery** count interactions through those services. Look for seasonal patterns within each chart. Equal line heights on different charts do not mean equal activity.

Repeat visits count separately. Different services may report each location type, so a rise can reflect a change in reporting as well as use. Flat stretches may be totals spread evenly across months; gaps mean missing data, not no activity.
