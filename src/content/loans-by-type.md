Each chart shows monthly loans for one format per 1,000 residents in areas reporting that format and age category. Look for seasonal patterns. Check the scales before comparing charts.

**Physical books** show borrowing of adult and children's books. **Ebooks** and **Eaudio** show digital reading and listening. **Physical audiobooks** show loans of recorded audio on physical media.

Age groups refer to the item's intended audience, not the borrower's age. **Unknown** means no age breakdown was supplied. Rates use the whole reporting population, not just children or adults. Different services may report each category or month, so changes do not always mean demand changed. Flat stretches may be quarterly or yearly totals spread evenly across months; gaps mean missing data, not zero loans.
