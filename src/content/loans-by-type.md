See monthly loans per 1,000 residents of services reporting each format and age group: adult or all-ages, young people (12–17), and children (under 12). Age groups describe the item's intended audience, not the borrower's age. Items without an age category count as adult or all-ages. **Unknown** is the reported format total not accounted for by the available age-band detail; when no usable age detail is reported, the full total is Unknown. Source totals are used to calculate this remainder, not added as a second age category. Zero age-band values do not by themselves prove that a breakdown is complete. Unknown can change when reporting detail changes, not only when loan activity changes.
