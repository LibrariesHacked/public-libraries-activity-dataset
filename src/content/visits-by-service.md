Compare services or regions for each location type. Charts show visits or interactions per 1,000 residents in areas with reported data, sorted by rate. Rates use the reported months to estimate a full year. Separate charts keep smaller services visible and avoid adding rates based on different reporting areas.

Regional rates include only services reporting that location type. Geography, where visitors travel from and counting methods can affect the figures. Missing rates are left out, not treated as zero. Check unusual bars against the monthly charts and source data.
