An active user is a member who borrowed or renewed at least one physical or digital item during the financial year. People who only visit, attend events or use computers are not counted.

Cards use the latest selected financial year, regardless of the months selected. Counts are not added across years: the same person may borrow in several years or belong to more than one service. Age-group counts may not add to the total because of unknown ages or missing data.

Percentages compare borrowers with residents in areas that reported data. Changes are in percentage points, not growth in borrower numbers. One percentage point means one more borrower per 100 residents. These rates cover borrowing, not all library use.
